A long-path opening on 20m caught me by accident on a Sunday evening.
Worked VK4, ZL2, and a couple of JA stations on SSB inside an hour, all
with 50 watts into an end-fed wire at twelve metres. The grey-line was
doing the work; I just had to be there.

The signal report I'm proudest of is 57 from VK4 — nothing dramatic,
just clean copy on both sides. The grey line moved through us at about
17:40 UTC; by 18:20 the band had collapsed back to Europe.

{{< note >}}
For anyone starting out: a horizontal end-fed half-wave is not
glamorous, but with a 49:1 unun and 18 m of decent coax it'll work
multiple bands and get you on the air. Worry about the tower later.
{{< /note >}}

## Conditions

| | |
|---|---|
| SFI | 168 |
| A index | 9 |
| K index | 2 |
| Mode | SSB |
| Power | 50 W |
| Path | Long, EU → VK |

If you're chasing similar openings, watch the [grey-line
map](https://www.dxmaps.com/) and be patient. It's a hobby, not a job.
