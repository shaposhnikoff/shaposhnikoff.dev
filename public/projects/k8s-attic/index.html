What it is

A small controller that finds Kubernetes resources nobody loves anymore — deployments scaled to zero for 30+ days, PVCs unbound, ConfigMaps with no references — and posts a polite weekly digest to Slack.

Why it exists

Cluster sprawl is real and silent. Nothing pages, but six months later your cluster has 800 unused ConfigMaps and three leaked LoadBalancers quietly costing $40 each.
