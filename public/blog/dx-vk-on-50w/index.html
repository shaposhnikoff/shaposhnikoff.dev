Working an Australian station from Europe with 50 watts

A long-path opening on 20m caught me by accident on a Sunday evening. Worked VK4, ZL2, and a couple of JA stations on SSB inside an hour, all with 50 watts into an end-fed wire at twelve metres. The grey-line was doing the work; I just had to be there.

The signal report I’m proudest of is 57 from VK4 — nothing dramatic, just clean copy on both sides. The grey line moved through us at about 17:40 UTC; by 18:20 the band had collapsed back to Europe.

// note
For anyone starting out: a horizontal end-fed half-wave is not glamorous, but with a 49:1 unun and 18 m of decent coax it’ll work multiple bands and get you on the air. Worry about the tower later.

Conditions

SFI168
A index9
K index2
ModeSSB
Power50 W
PathLong, EU → VK

If you’re chasing similar openings, watch the grey-line map and be patient. It’s a hobby, not a job.
