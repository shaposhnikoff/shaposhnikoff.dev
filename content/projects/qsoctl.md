## What it is

A keyboard-only logbook for the bands. Looks like `ranger`, behaves like
`tmux`, exports ADIF, uploads to LoTW with a single keystroke.

## Status

Experimental. I use it daily; I would not recommend it to anyone who
hasn't read the source.
